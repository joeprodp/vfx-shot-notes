# VFX Shot Notes

On-set VFX shot notes for iPad and iPhone, laid out like a US Legal Notability sheet.

- One page per setup; **Next setup** carries everything forward in blue italics until you change it
- **All shots** directory grouped by shoot day, with search
- Number-first boxes with an in-box ABC / 123 switch
- PDF export (one shot or the whole day) to the iOS share sheet
- Works offline; all data stays on the device

**Install:** open the site in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Always launch from the icon.
